One Bridge, Two Payloads

Redrawn for the current build: a bridge queue on Broker A is what actually crosses the shaped, flooded link, for both use cases in the Field Manual. Video and sensor telemetry share the same mechanism — they just get their own queue and topic.

Companion to the Tactical Field Manual. Replaces this artifact's earlier diagrams, which showed the retired BroadcastMe/bare-bridge topology.

Part One

Video

The direct-UDP comparison and the Solace-bridged RTSP path run at once, over the same shaped interface — which is exactly why both share one fate at the queue, and only one of them has a way back.

Part Two

Sensor Fusion

Same skeleton, smaller payload, sharper stakes: a synthetic drone-track generator standing in for a 5G network-sensing site, publishing over MQTT to the same Broker A, with its own bridge queue so it never shares a spool with video.

What's the same, what's different Same shaper, same 768 kbit cap, same promotion-and-bridge mechanism. What changes between the two parts is only the topic, the queue name, and the transport the client uses to publish (SMF direct vs. MQTT QoS 0) — the broker doesn't care which; both get promoted the same way.
